# CONTEXT.md — Team Leadership 2026

## What this workspace is

*This is where the fam is applied to team leadership for a Product Support
(Frontline) team in the AI-powered support era — hiring, coaching, team
communications, and team performance. The four-stage framework below is the
workspace's operational shape.*

Rosie is a Product Support lead doing informal L4 leadership work on a small,
remote team: supporting the team's manager on hiring, mentoring peers through
coaching and feedback, drafting team communications, and driving visibility
on team performance. Every session produces a concrete artifact — a candidate
evaluation, a coaching plan, a team update, or a performance read — ready to
act on or hand to the manager.

## How the work is shaped

Four stages, one folder each. Each has its own trigger and output shape; they don't chain.

| Stage | Folder | When to use | Output |
|-------|--------|-------------|--------|
| **01 — Hiring** | `stages/01_hiring/` | Interview prep, candidate evaluation, hire recommendation | A per-competency scorecard with evidence plus a narrative summary |
| **02 — Coaching** | `stages/02_coaching/` | 1:1 prep, mentee development, skill-gap work, feedback drafting | A coaching plan, feedback draft, or development note |
| **03 — Team comms** | `stages/03_team_comms/` | Team updates, async messages, knowledge sharing, announcements | A ready-to-post draft in the team's channel format |
| **04 — Team performance** | `stages/04_team_performance/` | Team health reviews, performance insights, recommendations for the manager | A performance read or insight artifact, shareable with the manager |

Sessions operate in one stage at a time.

## Approval gates

| What | Who approves |
|------|--------------|
| Hiring decisions and offer recommendations | Rosie's manager |
| Coaching feedback delivered to a mentee | Rosie (owns peer coaching directly) |
| Team communications posted under team identity | Rosie's manager (for cross-team announcements); Rosie (for in-team updates) |
| Performance reads shared externally | Rosie's manager |

Rosie supports, advises, and prepares. Final calls on hiring and cross-team
positioning sit with the manager. This is the same split that shows up
elsewhere in the fam: the owner-of-the-decision is not always the author of
the artifact that prepares the decision.

## Why the architecture looks like this

Team leadership work is heterogeneous by default. Hiring is episodic, coaching
is recurring, team comms are on-demand, and performance is reviewed against
quarterly rhythms. A stage-based shape matches that reality — each stage owns
its own trigger, artifact shape, and review cadence, and none of them forces
the others onto its clock.

The load-bearing artifacts are the "main character" performance standard
(insight-sharing, continuous learning, creative problem-solving, AI-era
readiness, team contribution — the definition of "good" that drives all four
stages) and the hiring output format rule (scorecard plus narrative, never one
or the other). Those artifacts are the brain; the stages are the arms and legs.

The fam runs the same governance protocols here (P1–P8, checkpoint bar, state
updates) as in every other workspace. What's workspace-specific is that the
outputs are almost always for a human decision-maker — the manager, a mentee,
or the team — so tone and sign-off discipline matter more than format.
