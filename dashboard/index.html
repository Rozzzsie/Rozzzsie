Meta-finding
This cycle's findings share one shape: a published or relied-on number that was copied forward instead of derived from the thing it counts. The ritual dial's denominator came from a prose list of sixteen step labels, while the ritual has had seventeen since a step was added in September, so five cycles published sixteen of sixteen with that step never counted. A count of the stop gate's checks was carried as six for five cycles, against eighteen checks in the gate, under a backlog row that already said to stop carrying it. The metrics tally again counted mentions of a launch command as launches. The hook audit again read a benign skip as an error, because its error vocabulary was written for one hook and applies to all. Each figure was honest about where it came from and wrong about what it measured. This cycle derives the two counts from the structure they count and puts the producer fixes on ledgers.
