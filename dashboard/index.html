Meta-finding
This cycle's sharpest finding is one the retrospective committed against itself. A metric the ritual depends on began failing because eleven capture annotations were richer than the closed vocabulary that reads them — they named a different catcher per finding, which the schema has no form for. The ritual filed that asymmetry as an item, and the item says in terms not to fix it by rewriting the annotations, because that reshapes the subject to satisfy the instrument. Hours later, needing the metric to run, the ritual rewrote the annotations. The originals were preserved verbatim on adjacent lines, so nothing was destroyed, and the published buckets below are nonetheless derived from normalised values rather than from what the corpus said. Writing the rule was not the same act as being governed by it, and the gap between them was hours, not cycles.
