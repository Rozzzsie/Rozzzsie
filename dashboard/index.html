Meta-finding
This cycle's findings share one shape: a surface that teaches or records a form, read by a party that takes the form to mean something it does not. The intent-confirmation template showed a skip line for explicit instructions, and the stop gate blocks exactly that line at three or more productive files, so four sessions followed the template and could not close. A team-set label in a ticket export was read nine times as its meaning, and the operator corrected each one. The metrics tally again counted mentions of a launch command as 306 launches. Insight cards record the folder a session ran from, which is the same folder for every workspace, so the field cannot say which workspace a card came from. In each case the form was faithful and the reading was wrong. The template, the label rule and the close rule were fixed this cycle; the gate half, the tally and the card field are on ledgers.
