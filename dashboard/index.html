Meta-finding
This cycle's sharpest findings were records that answered confidently about their own state and were wrong. A proposal ledger kept eight items marked pending for four to five months; one of them had already been superseded in its own lifecycle log, and no backlog row tracked the rest, so nothing surfaced them. The hook audit read two healthy hooks as failing because its list of result words is kept by hand. A rule against unverified "this is new" claims existed only in a reference file whose own header calls that a defect, and five such claims were made in a week. A push-safety rule fired only after a block, while the pushes that published another session's work followed no block at all. In each case the rule or record existed and was correct about something; it was attached to a place or a trigger the act never passed through. Each was moved to where the act happens this cycle, or filed where a reader will see it.
