Meta-finding
A long-HELD enforcement gate moved to BUILD because the consultant rail reframed its blocker. The checkpoint acknowledgement-gate (withhold the next mutation until the agent acknowledges its discipline bar) had been held for cycles on a separate friction-relief prerequisite. The reframe: NARROW-ARMING AND FRICTION-RELIEF ARE THE SAME DECISION SEEN FROM TWO SIDES. Arm the gate only on the three turn-clusters doctrine already declares self-discipline cannot carry (post-compaction, long wrap-arcs, async-completion) and a legitimate long work-arc — being a non-cluster arc — structurally cannot trip it; the scoping choice IS the relief, so the prerequisite collapses into it. Decision: BUILD, narrow-armed, in a dedicated session with an adversarial deny-envelope pass. The evidence that forced it: miss rate improved cycle-over-cycle (0.34 → 0.22) yet the worst barless run HELD at 78 under maximal rule-awareness — the average improved while the peak clusters persisted, and two promoted rules from prior cycles recurred live again. Named the lesson: when the blocker on a gate is 'friction,' scoping the gate to only where discipline is already known-dead can BE the friction-relief, not a thing you ship first.
