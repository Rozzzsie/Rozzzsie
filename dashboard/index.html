Meta-finding
AN AUDIT STEP THAT NAMES THE OBLIGATION AND NOT THE INSTRUMENT IS RE-IMPLEMENTED BY HAND EVERY CYCLE, AND THE HAND-ROLLED INSTRUMENT IS WORSE THAN THE ONE ALREADY IN THE TREE. Two of this ritual's ten steps mandate a sweep and name no parser. Three parsers for the same format already exist in the codebase and all three are correct. The ritual used a fourth, invented at the point of use, which lost roughly 40% of the corpus — and the loss was not uniform: it silently dropped three workspaces to zero on a step whose own text says "all active workspaces." The sharper half is the direction. A hand-rolled matcher that under-counts returns a PLAUSIBLE NON-ZERO, and a non-zero is its own positive control — it proves the file was reached, the tool ran, and the pattern compiled. So it passes affirmatively and nothing invites a re-check. Every zero-keyed tripwire in this architecture is blind to it by construction. What caught it was not a control but a CONTRADICTION: one fact known about the corpus without the matcher. And the same step failed in the opposite direction on the same cycle. Its second audit tripwire names no corpus AND has no affirmative token, so "never observed = regression" is true by construction — unfalsifiable, and it would have published a regression that does not exist. The independent check showed 77% compliance. Both failures are one defect: the step specifies WHAT to conclude and not WHAT to read.
